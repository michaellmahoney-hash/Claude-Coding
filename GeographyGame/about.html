What is GeoQuest?

GeoQuest is an interactive geography quiz game that helps players of all ages sharpen their knowledge of world cities and their locations. Each round, a city name is displayed and you must click as close as possible to it on the interactive world map. The closer your click, the more points you earn.

The game is designed to be both educational and entertaining — a quick way to test yourself, challenge a friend, or simply explore the world one city at a time. GeoQuest is entirely free to play with no registration required.

How to Play

  1. Choose your number of rounds and difficulty level on the start screen, then click Start Game.
  2. A city name appears at the top of the screen. Use your geography knowledge to find it on the world map.
  3. Click the map where you think the city is located. The closer your click to the real location, the more points you earn.
  4. After each round, see how far off you were and how many points you scored. The real city location is revealed on the map.
  5. After all rounds, view your final score, letter grade, and a full breakdown of every city. Try again to beat your best!

Scoring System

Points are awarded based on how close your guess is to the actual city location. The maximum score per round is 1,000 points. A perfect click on the city earns full marks; guesses farther away earn proportionally fewer points.

Distance from city Points earned Rating
Under 50 km 850 – 1,000 Excellent
50 – 300 km 500 – 850 Good
300 – 800 km 200 – 500 Close
800 – 2,000 km 50 – 200 Far
Over 2,000 km 0 – 50 Very Far

Difficulty Levels

GeoQuest offers three difficulty levels to suit all skill levels:

🟢

Easy

Well-known world capitals and major cities. Great for beginners or younger players.

🟡

Medium

A mix of major and regional cities from all continents. The default challenge.

🔴

Hard

Includes smaller cities and less-familiar locations. A real test of geographic mastery.

Why Play GeoQuest?

Geography knowledge is more useful than ever in a connected world. Understanding where countries, cities, and regions are located helps with:

Global awareness — Following world news is much easier when you know where events are happening. GeoQuest builds an intuitive mental map of the world.

Education — Students can use GeoQuest to study for geography tests, explore continents they're learning about, and make memorizing city locations genuinely fun.

Travel planning — Knowing where cities are in relation to each other helps travelers understand distances and plan itineraries more effectively.

Competitive fun — Challenge friends or family to beat your high score. Each playthrough is different thanks to the randomized city selection.

Technology

GeoQuest is built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no heavy dependencies. The interactive map is powered by Leaflet.js with OpenStreetMap tiles. The game runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.
