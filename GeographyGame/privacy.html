Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Welcome to GeoQuest ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit and play GeoQuest at claude-coding.vercel.app. By using our site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

GeoQuest does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to play. We do not collect your name, email address, or any directly identifying data.

However, certain information may be collected automatically:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, browser type, device type, and general geographic region (country/city level) via standard web server logs.
  • Cookies: Third-party advertising partners (Google AdSense) may set cookies on your device to serve relevant advertisements. See Section 3 for details.

2. How We Use Information

Any automatically collected data is used only to:

  • Understand how players interact with the game to improve it
  • Ensure the site is functioning correctly
  • Display advertisements that help fund free access to GeoQuest

We do not sell, rent, or trade any information to third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Google AdSense & Advertising

GeoQuest uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

For more information on how Google collects and processes data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. GeoQuest itself does not set any first-party cookies. Cookies placed on your device come exclusively from our third-party advertising partner (Google AdSense). You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

5. Children's Privacy

GeoQuest is a general-audience geography game suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

6. Third-Party Links

GeoQuest does not contain links to third-party websites beyond those required for game functionality (such as the Leaflet map library). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party services.

7. Data Security

Since GeoQuest does not collect personal data directly, there is no personal information stored on our servers. All game state (scores, rounds) is processed locally in your browser and is not transmitted to or stored on our servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of GeoQuest after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us by opening an issue on our project repository or through the contact information associated with the site owner.
